> 5. "Please read page 23, line 7. Do you believe a Flight Control Operator should be able to generate reports for an ACA he/she doesn't work in? Wrong question, wasn't it?"

## US080 — Flight Plan, Fuel and Legs

**Question (Fernandes 1241949):**

US080 lists "fuel quantity" as a single input field (alongside aircraft, date/time, pilot) and does not mention legs. The DSL semantic rules (3.4.5) say fuel must be "associated with a specific leg." How should the fuel quantity entered in US080 reconcile with the fuel per leg in a flight plan file?

Regarding legs, section 3.2 says "Only direct flights will be considered," but section 3.4.1 says a flight "is composed of one or more flight legs." Should US080 model flights closer to the DSL (a flight holds multiple legs, fuel per leg), or keep US080 flat (one fuel value, no leg structure)?

And is the "only direct flights" simplification about what the system stores, or only for simulation purposes?

**Answer (Angelo Martins):**

> "Really? Have you read section 3.4.3? You have fuel quantity and the departure time for each leg."
>
> "And of course you are not suggesting that we should store the fuel quantity both in the Flight domain entity and in the FlightPlan description (DSL). The fuel quantity is used only for the simulation."
>
> "Regarding departure time for the flight (1st leg), I'm torn between doing it right and doing the right thing. Choosing the later, in spite of having the information in the FlightPlan description (DSL), I believe departure time of the first leg is a property of a flight and one that will be used often. Thus, I would say that I would propose a duplication here, albeit with a verification in US085, so that incongruent information is detected."
>
> "A student doesn't fail due to skipping client's classes, but these classes are not optional."

## US075 — Register Pilot

**Question (Dias 1210126):**

1. What should the format of the pilot licence number be?
2. Regarding the skills assessment date, should the system allow only past or present dates, or also future dates for scheduled assessments? Should there be a maximum age restriction (e.g., 12 months)? Is this field mandatory for every pilot?
3. Regarding certified aircraft models:
   - 3a) Should a pilot be required to have at least one certification at creation time, or can it be empty initially?
   - 3b) Should there be a maximum number of certifications?
   - 3c) Should certifications be removable or only additive for audit purposes?
4. Regarding the relationship between pilot and air transport company: cardinality 1:N (pilot belongs to exactly one company) or N:M (pilot can be associated with multiple companies)?
5. Should any additional attributes be stored beyond the current scope (licence issue date, expiration date, nationality, base airport, flight hours, status)?

**Answer (Angelo Martins):**

> 1. "Where is the pilot's licence mentioned in the requirements document? You are making things up."
> 2. "Page 8, lines 7 and 8. You don't schedule things in the past, thus you either have a skills' assessment (in the past) or you don't have any. Skills' assessment applies to all users, not just pilots. You must have at least one skills's assessment in order to register a new user? Let's say yes."
> 3. "US075, page 18, line 29, it is very clear: 'A pilot is certified to pilot one or more aircraft models.' 3b is nonsense and 3c is out of the scope of the project."
> 4. "US075 is very clear: a pilot works for an ATC. If he/she could work for more than one, then adding a pilot would have to be independent of the company. That's not the case."
> 5. "Stick to the project requirements. You mentioning that flight hours, a calculated field, could be an attribute gives me a full allergic reaction. I believe ChatGPT wouldn't say that."
